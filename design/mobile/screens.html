Family Tree on a phone

Every screen, surface and conditional state from the desktop catalog, re-laid for one hand. The sidebar becomes a bottom bar for the four views you move between plus a drawer for everything else; the detail panel, every menu and every dialog become bottom sheets; the canvas stays a real pan-and-zoom surface with a floating toolbar. Palette, type and elevation are unchanged — only density, reach and hit targets are.
